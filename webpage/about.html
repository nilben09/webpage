About us

Samson Polytechnic College of Davao serves students from high school through college. Our teachers combine hands-on instruction with personal guidance so every student is ready for work or further study.

Mission

To provide quality, affordable education for everyone.

Vision

Capable, principled students who love their country.

Values

Honesty, hard work, respect, and community.
